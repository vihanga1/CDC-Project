OUR LEGACY

A legacy built on community dedication

For over two decades, CDC has worked in the hills of Ussapitiya, building food security systems, training farmers, and preserving plant biodiversity. This sustained dedication has been recognised by leading national institutions celebrating women who drive meaningful change across Sri Lanka.

From food security advocacy to social service leadership, each award affirms what CDC has always believed — that grassroots, community-driven development creates lasting impact.

FEATURED INITIATIVE

Protecting Sri Lanka’s food biodiversity

CDC’s “Lesser-Known Foods of Sri Lanka” initiative documents and promotes nutritious native plant foods disappearing from modern diets. By training communities in their cultivation, preservation, and preparation, CDC is restoring both biodiversity and nutritional resilience across the hill country.
