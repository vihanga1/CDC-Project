Women’s Livelihood

Rebuilding after loss, rising with strength

Ranjani was building her livelihood through food production and small enterprise when the Dittawa storm damaged her home and disrupted her life. With CDC’s guidance and support, she found the strength to begin again. Today, she continues rebuilding her family’s future through courage, work, and community enterprise.

R.M. Ranjani Ratnayake

Community Enterprise

Finding confidence through self-employment

Dhammika once faced many barriers in growing her self-employment work. When CDC came to her village, she found encouragement, guidance, and practical support. Step by step, she strengthened her skills, gained confidence, and continued her journey as a proud community entrepreneur.

Dhammika Kumari
