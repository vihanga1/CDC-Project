Stories of Change

Behind every programme is a person, a family, a turning point. These are stories of how lives begin to change when opportunity meets support.

All stories

Women’s livelihood

From learning a skill to leading others

After joining our training programme, she started with a small idea. Today she earns her own income and helps other women in her village do the same. What began as a single workshop has grown into a network of confidence and self-reliance.

Sustainable agriculture

A family farm, transformed by knowledge

A smallholder family adopted organic practices through CDC’s model farm programme. Their yield improved and their soil recovered. Now they share what they learned with neighbouring farmers in the same valley.

Eco tourism

Opening their home, building an income

When CDC introduced community-based eco tourism to their village, one family decided to open their home to visitors. The connections they’ve made since have brought both income and a renewed pride in their land and culture.

Community development

When a village decides to act together

It started with a community meeting and a shared concern about water access. With CDC’s support, the village mapped their resources, formed a committee, and built a solution they now maintain themselves.

Local enterprise

Tradition on a table, turned into a livelihood

A group of women began preserving traditional recipes as part of CDC’s food production training. Their products now reach markets across the region, keeping local flavours alive while building genuine economic independence.

Conservation

Protecting the forest, protecting the future

A community that once cleared land to survive is now one of the most active stewards of their forest. Through CDC’s conservation programme, they found that protecting nature and building livelihoods are not opposing goals.
